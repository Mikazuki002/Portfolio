About Me

Building at the edge of games, code, and data.

I'm Alord Jhinroy M. Somera, a 3rd-year BS Computer Science student at Cavite State University. My technical interests span game development, software engineering, and data-driven systems — three domains I'm actively building toward.

My first serious project, Rank Zero, was built entirely in Godot Engine using GDScript — from game logic and physics to scene management and UI systems. It taught me that shipping something, even imperfect, is the fastest way to grow as an engineer.

I'm currently expanding into Python-based data analysis and machine learning fundamentals, with an internship on the horizon in mid-2025. I believe the best software engineers are T-shaped: deep in one area, broad enough to contribute across the stack.
